# 176 Triggered mana abilities: decision report

Grilling ticket, run with the user over three rounds of `AskUserQuestion`. The main thread wrote every question and recommendation. The user picked every answer.

## Facts gathered before the interview

- 51 printed cards have a triggered mana ability (the Decision 10 query, which counts distinct cards: 54 rows, 54 distinct `oracle_id`s, 3 of them adding no mana). The two big families are the Wild Growth auras ("Whenever enchanted land is tapped for mana, its controller adds an additional {G}") and "Whenever you tap a [type] for mana, add ..." (Nirkana Revenant, Vernal Bloom, Mirari's Wake, Kinnan). Query: `oracle_text` matching "tapped for mana", "tap ... for mana" or "mana ability" with "add", in `../stackfish-db/stackfish.db`.
- Mana Reflection, which the ticket names, is not a trigger. "If you tap a permanent for mana, it produces twice as much" is a replacement effect (CR 106.12b). Contamination, Pulse of Llanowar, Mirri and Hall of Gemstone are replacements too.
- `GameEventKind::ManaAdded` (`types/event.rs:56`) carries `player` and `units` but no source, so no condition can tell that a permanent was tapped for mana (CR 106.12a). `ActivatePlan::FreeMana` emits `Tapped` and `ManaAdded` (`commands/activate.rs:494-524`). The paid path adds the mana in `engine/payment.rs:179-197`.
- A mana ability activated mid-payment suspends the payment and runs a full pipeline run, then resumes the payment (`engine/action.rs`, `triggers_pipeline.rs:95-98`). A probe test (a `BecomesTapped` trigger on a land, the land tapped mid-payment for a sorcery) ended with `stack = [Spell, TriggeredAbility]` while still `AwaitingPayment`. The engine puts an ordinary trigger fired by a mana ability on the stack before the spell is cast, which CR 601.2i and 603.3 forbid. I deleted the probe.
- The engine has no standalone color-choice Decision. Colors are announced on `ActivateAbility.chosen_colors` and listed through `ColorChoice` on the entry (`decision/entry.rs:95`).
- The TODO at `commands/activate.rs:249` already notes that a triggered mana ability's mana is not tagged snow.

## Decisions

| # | Question | Options | Choice | By | Audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What 176 covers | Triggers only; triggers and replacements | Triggers only. Mana-production replacements (Mana Reflection, Contamination, Pulse of Llanowar) become a Not yet specified item | User, on the main thread's recommendation | Approved |
| 2 | How the engine knows a permanent was tapped for mana | New event; widen `ManaAdded` with a source; pair `Tapped` with `ManaAdded` | New `ManaAbilityResolved { source, ability, player, units, tapped }` emitted beside `ManaAdded` whenever an activated mana ability resolves, on both the free and the paid path. `tapped` is true when the activation tapped its own source as a cost (CR 106.12): a `TapSelf` cost, or the no-cost shape. The no-cost shape always taps. `commands/activate.rs:262-283` rejects it on a tapped source or a summoning-sick creature, which are the {T} checks, and `ActivatePlan::FreeMana` sets `tapped = true`. A printed mana ability without {T} has some other cost, such as a sacrifice or mana, so it never takes the no-cost shape. A `{0}` cost is written `:AddCost(Game.Costs.Mana("{0}"))`, and `cost.mana` being `Some` sends it down the paid path, not the no-cost one (`activate.rs:262`). Vivi Ornitier is the only printed `{0}: Add` card. The test is the same one the CONTEXT entry uses. A triggered mana ability emits only `ManaAdded`, so it cannot set off another "tapped for mana" trigger | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 3 | Where a triggered mana ability resolves | Split at the collect stage; inline in the activation command | Split at collect. When the pipeline collects triggers, matches that are triggered mana abilities resolve at once, before anything is pushed. The rest go on the stack as before. One rule covers activation at priority and mid-payment. A triggered mana ability's own resolution emits only `ManaAdded`, which `TappedForMana` never matches, so in the first build nothing chains. The second clause of CR 605.1b (mana being added) can be added at the same split later | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 4 | Order when one activation triggers several | Fixed order; APNAP with an order Decision | Fixed order: ability registration (timestamp) order, with no Decision. CR 603.3b would let each controller order their own triggers, but these never touch the stack and their mana adds up, so in the first build's families the order changes nothing that matters except which color Decision comes first. The build carries a TODO for it | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 5 | How the player picks a color for "one mana of any color" | New Decision mid-run; announce trigger colors on `ActivateAbility` | A new `ChooseManaColors` Decision for the trigger's controller lists the allowed colors and count. Announcing early would make the listing predict Lua conditions | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 6 | What the Payment Decision shows about the extra mana | Nothing new; an expected-output hint per mana ability | Nothing new. The extra mana shows in the pool after activation, in line with 003's rule of no affordability checks in Decisions | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 7 | The mid-payment trigger bug from the probe | Separate ticket; fold into the 176 build | Separate build ticket. The 176 build only splits off triggered mana abilities and does not wait for it | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 8 | How the engine tells a triggered mana ability from an ordinary trigger | Dedicated Lua builder; engine classifies by CR 605.1b | `Game.CreateTriggeredManaAbility(c)`, known at load time like `Game.CreateManaAbility`. It has no `:SetTarget`, its condition must be a mana-event tag and its operation must add mana. Breaking any of these is a load error at `:Register()`, as `Game.CreateManaAbility` does for a non-mana operation. In the first build the only mana-event tag is `TappedForMana`. A trigger that adds no mana, such as Tyvar's, stays on `Game.CreateTriggeredAbility` (CR 605.5a). A Wild Growth script: `local e = Game.CreateTriggeredManaAbility(c)`, `e:SetCondition(Game.Conditions.TappedForMana({ object = "Enchanted", controller = "Any" }))`, `e:SetOperation(Game.Ops.AddMana("G"))`, `e:Register()` | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 9 | The Lua condition shape | One tag with an option bag; several fixed tags; custom function only | `Game.Conditions.TappedForMana({ object = "Enchanted" \| "Self" \| filter, controller = "You" \| "Any", produced = "G" })`. The filter is the usual `FilterSpec` table. `controller` defaults to `"Any"`, so Wild Growth also fires when an opponent taps the enchanted land. `produced` covers Gauntlet of Power | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 10 | Who gets the mana, and "mana of any type that land produced" | Event player plus a new op; trigger controller plus a new op | Inside a triggered mana ability, `AddMana` and `AddManaChoice` add to the player who activated the triggering ability ("its controller"), which is right for an opponent's Vernal Bloom. The query `select ... from cards where (oracle_text like '%Whenever%tap%for mana%' or oracle_text like '%Whenever%tapped for mana%') and oracle_text like '%add%'` returns 54 rows. 51 say "that player adds", "its controller adds", "its controller may add", ", add" or ", you add". The other 3 (Forbidden Orchard, Zhur-Taa Druid, Storm Cauldron) add no mana from the trigger. Every ", add" row is "Whenever you tap ..." (Nirkana Revenant, Crypt Ghast, Roxanne's Meteorite, Selesnya Loft Gardens) or "this land is tapped" (Vigorous Farming), where only the trigger's controller can tap the land. The rows show the mana always going to the player who tapped: "that player adds" (Mana Flare, High Tide), "its controller adds" (Wild Growth), or "you add" with the tapper also being "you" (Nirkana Revenant). No card makes the trigger's controller add mana when someone else taps, so no second recipient option is needed. A new `Game.Ops.AddManaOfProducedType(n)` reads the event's units and asks through `ChooseManaColors` only when there are several types | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 11 | First build scope | Core families; Wild Growth only; all of them | Core families: fixed color, any color and produced type, on "enchanted", "this" or a filtered permanent. Mana is snow when the triggered ability's source is snow (CR 107.4h, which points to 106.3), which clears the TODO at `activate.rs:249`. Wild Growth and Fertile Ground join the proof deck | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 12 | Bot and wire for `ChooseManaColors` | New kind at version 1 with a needs-based bot; bump the protocol | A new Decision kind and Answer, `PROTOCOL_VERSION` stays 1. An old client is not expected to handle the unknown kind. `crates/host/src/envelope.rs:8-11` keeps the version at 1, even for breaking changes, until the first tagged `crates/host` release or the first host outside this repository. Every current client (the bot, the replay file and the Godot adapter) lives in this repository, so the build updates all of them in the same change. The bot picks a color the open payment still needs, else the first allowed color | User, on recommendation | Approved |
| 13 | Does `TappedForMana` work on ordinary triggers | Same tag on both builders; mana builder only | Same tag on both. On `Game.CreateTriggeredAbility` it goes on the stack like any trigger. Zhur-Taa Druid ("{T}: Add {G}. Whenever you tap this creature for mana, it deals 1 damage to each opponent.") adds no mana from the trigger, and Forbidden Orchard's trigger targets, so both are ordinary triggers under CR 605.5a. Zhur-Taa Druid is the build's test for it, activated at priority. Mid-payment timing for ordinary triggers belongs to the hold ticket | User, on recommendation | Approved |

- "May add": Snowfall.
- Triggers from mana being added rather than from a mana ability (the second clause of CR 605.1b).
- Tyvar's "whenever a mana ability of this creature resolves" condition.
- Regal Behemoth's "while you're the monarch" needs an intervening-if on the mana builder.
- `// TODO: each controller orders their simultaneous triggered mana abilities (CR 603.3b)`.
